Pick a spot on the wall

Small groups, sorted by age, so a five-year-old gets to splash and a twelve-year-old gets to properly learn how light works.

How it goes

  1. 1

    Come for a trial

    One class, no commitment. Your child paints something to take home and you both see if it clicks.

  2. 2

    Join for the term

    Same time each weekend. Every term has a theme, a new medium and one painting they'll be really proud of.

  3. 3

    Hang it up

    Term ends with a little exhibition. Family comes, work goes on the wall, artists take a bow.
